When to prune, feed, and spray your Michigan fruit trees

Pick your tree and where you grow it. You'll get the best timing for pruning, fertilizing, and disease sprays, with today's date marked on each calendar.

Which tree do you have?

Timing is based on general Michigan State University Extension guidance for home orchards, which tracks tree growth stages (green tip, bloom, petal fall) more than calendar dates. A warm spring can move everything up by two weeks or more, so watch your buds and use the calendar as a guide.

For spray products and rates, check MSU Extension and the current label on any product you buy. Labels are the law, and rules differ for home and commercial use. Keep insecticides off open blooms to protect pollinators.
